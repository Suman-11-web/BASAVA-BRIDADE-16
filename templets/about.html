The Driving Force

ಬಸವ ಬ್ರಿಗೇಡ್ ಹದಿನಾರು

Founded with a vision to unite the youth of Hadinaru, Basava Brigade is the vibrant organizing force behind this grand Ganeshotsava. What started as a small group of passionate youngsters has now grown into a dedicated community.

Our focus extends beyond just the festival. We are dedicated to cultural preservation, spiritual devotion, and community welfare. Through our official Instagram handle, we document our journey, showcase our rich village traditions, and connect with devotees globally.

  • Youth Empowerment & Unity
  • Preserving Traditional Values
  • Dedicated Community Service

Eco-Friendly Idol

Committed to nature conservation, our Ganesha idol is crafted from 100% pure clay (Shadu Maati) and natural colors, ensuring a safe and eco-friendly Visarjan.

ಮಹಾ ಅನ್ನದಾಸೋಹ

Maha Anna Dasoha

A grand community feast organized for all devotees. We believe in the philosophy of Dasoha, serving thousands of people regardless of their background with devotion and love.

Cultural Heritage

Evenings filled with divine Bhajans, traditional folk dances, and talent shows highlighting the vibrant culture and artistry of Hadinaru village youth.
